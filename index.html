About Burger Rush

Burger Rush is a cooking game where you make burgers for a line of hungry animal customers. Each customer shows their order on a ticket: a bun, a patty (beef, chicken, veggie or fish), cheese, toppings and a sauce. Tap the ingredients in the same order as the ticket, and grill each patty until its cooking bar reaches the green zone. Wait too long and it burns!

Every customer has a patience bar, so serve them before it runs out. Quick, correct burgers with perfect patties earn the biggest tips. Reach the coin goal to win up to three stars and open the next day. Spend coins on a hotter grill, more grill space, a warming lamp, a tip jar and fun decorations, or add new ingredients to the menu. Orders grow bigger as the days go by, and your progress is saved on this device.

How to play

1. Read the ticket. Each customer wants one burger. The bubble above them shows a picture of it, and the ticket lists every part in order.

2. Grill the patty. Tap a patty in the fridge to put it on the grill. A bar under it fills up as it cooks. Tap the patty when the marker is in the green zone for a perfect cook. If it reaches red, it burns: tap it to throw it away.

3. Stack the burger. Tap the bun first, then follow the ticket from top to bottom. A wrong tap does not go on the burger, but it makes the customer a little less happy.

4. Serve fast. When the last part is on, the top bun closes and the burger goes to the customer. The heart bar is their patience. Quick, correct burgers with perfect patties earn the biggest tips.

Fries and juice. From day 3 some customers also want fries, and from day 5 a juice. Tap the fryer, then tap again when the bar is in the golden zone to scoop. For juice, hold the tank (or tap to start and tap to stop) and stop on the green line. Ready sides wait on the Ready shelf and go out with the burger. Missing a side only makes the tip smaller.

5. Win stars. Fill the coin bar at the top. One star opens the next day. Three stars is a perfect day!

6. Shop. Spend coins on a hotter grill, more grill space, a faster fryer, a bigger fry basket, new juice flavors, decorations (customers wait longer) and new ingredients (customers pay more).

Tip: tap a customer to switch to their order. The bin button clears the plate.
